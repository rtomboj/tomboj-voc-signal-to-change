# Source discovery and validation

Use this reference after the company profile is confirmed.

## Search from the company outward

Create search terms from the confirmed name, aliases, brands, products, locations, countries, and languages. Search for combinations such as:

- `[company or brand] reviews`
- `[product] customer reviews`
- `[company] complaints`
- `[company] Reddit` and `site:reddit.com [company or product]`
- `[company] forum`, `[company] community`, and `[product] discussion`
- `[company] [industry] directory`
- `[brand] [country or city] reviews`
- official app name plus `Apple App Store` or `Google Play`

Use search to discover category-specific sources rather than relying only on a fixed directory list.

## Likely source families

| Company profile | Sources worth testing |
| --- | --- |
| B2B software | G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights, Trustpilot, app marketplaces, Reddit, specialist communities |
| Consumer web/service | Trustpilot, BBB, ConsumerAffairs, Sitejabber, marketplace reviews, Reddit, sector directories |
| Physical or multi-location | Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, local or sector directories |
| Mobile product | Apple App Store, Google Play, device/platform marketplaces, Reddit, product communities |
| Healthcare or professional service | Relevant regulated or professional directories, Google, sector marketplaces, consumer complaint sources |
| Hospitality/travel | Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, booking and delivery marketplaces, relevant forums |

These are prompts for research, not mandatory sources.

Employee reviews such as Glassdoor are not customer VOC. Exclude them unless the user explicitly asks for employee voice as a separate analysis.

## Validate profile identity

Use multiple signals where possible:

- official domain linked from the platform profile;
- matching company, brand, product, app, or publisher name;
- matching address, phone, service area, or location;
- matching logo and product description;
- official company site linking back to the profile;
- market, language, and category alignment;
- current activity and review subject matter consistent with the offering.

Assign:

- **Verified:** strong direct match;
- **Probable:** several matching signals but no definitive link;
- **Needs confirmation:** plausible but ambiguity could materially affect the benchmark;
- **Rejected:** wrong entity, market, product, or audience.

Only Verified and user-confirmed Probable profiles may enter the baseline.

## Assess usefulness

For each verified source, assess:

- relevance to the intended customer or user population;
- brand/product/location coverage;
- visible review or discussion volume;
- recency;
- whether company responses are visible;
- risk of duplicate syndication from another source;
- availability of structured measures;
- likely Week 2 access route.

Use **Core**, **Secondary**, **Watchlist**, or **Exclude**. Explain the recommendation in one sentence.

## Discussion sources

Reddit, forums, and public communities can reveal language and issues that rated-review sites miss. They are also noisy and may contain prospects, employees, competitors, or unidentified participants.

Keep them in a separate `Discussion` source type. Capture relevant thread counts, dates, communities, and URLs when feasible, but do not translate discussion activity into star ratings or claim it is representative.

## Sources checked but not found

Log meaningful negative discovery results. “No verified profile found” is different from “not checked.” Include the search date and any ambiguity that prevented confirmation.
