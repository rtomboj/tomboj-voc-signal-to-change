## Contributor rule

When third-party material is added later:

1. Record the project, author, URL, license, version or commit, and files affected.
2. Distinguish `Copied`, `Adapted`, and `Consulted only`.
3. Preserve license and copyright notices required by the source.
4. Do not copy from a repository without an explicit license or direct permission.
5. Prefer official platform documentation when implementing future source connectors.
